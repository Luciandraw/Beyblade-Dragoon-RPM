APP SYNC

Sync with the Beyblade X app.

One button does it all. Try every step in the emulator above first.

  1. 1
    CLICK

    Open the menu and pick Bluetooth

    On the READY screen click once to open the menu, click twice more to reach BLUETOOTH, then double-click to open it.

  2. 2
    2×

    Turn it on

    Double-click on the Bluetooth screen. The header shows ON and the screen says WAITING.

  3. 3
    HOLD

    Leave the Bluetooth screen

    Hold 0.9 s twice to return to READY. Bluetooth stays on.

  4. 4
    2×

    Add the pass in the app

    In the Beyblade X app add a new pass and pick BEYBLADE_TOOL01.

  5. 5
    HOLD

    Press as the app asks

    When the app asks to hold the button for 1 second, hold it on READY. When it asks for two presses, double-click. Calibration does not start while the app is connected.

  6. 6
    AUTO

    Launch and sync

    The screen shows CONNECTED. Each launch goes to the app by itself. The last 50 launches wait on the device while the app is away.

Bluetooth is off by default and the setting is remembered. The device does not sleep while the app is connected.
